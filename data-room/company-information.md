## About Pillar

Pillar is a property management SaaS platform that helps property managers deliver a modern, professional guest and tenant experience — without adding operational complexity. Built for the real-world demands of managing short-term rentals, mid-term furnished units, and residential properties, Pillar centralizes the digital touchpoints between managers and the people living in their properties.

At its core, Pillar gives every property a branded, mobile-first experience page that guests and tenants access via QR code or direct link. From that single page, residents can find property information, submit maintenance requests, chat with an AI assistant, and request late checkouts — all without calling or texting the manager. On the back end, managers get a clean dashboard to manage properties, track maintenance activity, customize content, and control their billing — from any device, at any time.

Pillar is a bootstrapped, founder-led business currently in active development and early customer deployment. The platform is live, fully functional, and serving property managers today.

## The Problem

Property management is operationally fragmented. Managers — whether overseeing 2 units or 200 — face the same recurring friction every day:

**1. Guests and tenants have nowhere to go for information.**
WiFi passwords, garage codes, checkout instructions, house rules, and local recommendations all live in scattered texts, emails, or paper printouts. When guests can't find them, they call or text the manager. For a manager with 10+ properties, these interruptions compound into hours of lost time each week.

**2. Maintenance and work orders are untracked.**
Most managers receive maintenance requests via text message. There is no record of what was submitted, no routing to the right vendor, no resolution tracking, and no audit trail. Issues fall through the cracks, and managers have no visibility into what is open across their portfolio at any given moment.

**3. Property branding is nonexistent.**
Short-term rental managers increasingly compete on guest experience, not just price. Yet the majority have no branded digital presence at the property level — no custom welcome page, no professional identity, nothing that distinguishes their property from a generic listing.

**4. Scaling without systems breaks everything.**
Adding properties to an unstructured workflow does not scale. Managers who grow beyond a handful of units hit a wall: too many text threads, too many lost details, no way to delegate or document effectively.

**5. Existing tools are overkill or misaligned.**
Enterprise property management software (AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi) is built for large operators with accounting and compliance needs. It is expensive, complex, and not designed for the guest experience layer. Nothing in the market elegantly solves the communication and presentation problems that everyday property managers face.

## Our Solution

Pillar solves these problems with two tightly integrated products: a **guest-facing property experience page** and a **manager portal** that powers it.

### The Property Experience Page
Each property gets a dedicated, mobile-first page that managers customize through their dashboard. Guests or tenants access it via QR code placed at the property or a shared link. The page displays:

- Property details: WiFi credentials, access codes, house rules, checkout instructions, and contact info — all in one place, accessible instantly
- A customizable digital guidebook (called **Windows**) with text, images, PDFs, and videos organized by room — replacing paper binders and scattered notes entirely
- An **AI Concierge** chatbot trained on the property's information, available 24/7 to answer guest questions about the property, local area, and logistics without involving the manager
- A **Work Order** submission tool that lets guests or tenants report maintenance issues directly from the property page, categorized and routed to the right contact automatically
- Late checkout request functionality, review prompts, and a photo gallery

The page is fully branded to the manager's identity — custom logo, hero image, color palette, and theme — making every property feel intentional and professional.

### The Manager Portal
Behind every property page is a full management dashboard where operators:

- Create and edit properties with a comprehensive form covering all content, branding, and configuration
- Monitor and resolve **work orders** across their entire portfolio from an activity dashboard with open-order counts, resolution notes, and history
- Set up **custom work order categories** with dedicated routing contacts (email and phone per category) so the right person gets the right notification automatically
- Generate **QR codes** for physical placement at each property
- Manage their subscription, property slots, and billing through an integrated Stripe portal
- Enable **multi-factor authentication** and control account security settings
- Track referrals and earn discounts for bringing other managers onto the platform

## Target Market

Pillar's primary customer is the **independent or small-portfolio property manager** — an operator managing between 1 and 50 residential units across short-term rentals, mid-term furnished rentals, or traditional residential properties. This segment is large, underserved by enterprise software, and highly motivated by tools that save time and elevate their professional image.

Secondary markets include boutique property management companies, co-host operators managing properties on behalf of owners, and real estate investors self-managing their own portfolios.

---

## Business Model

Pillar operates on a **SaaS subscription model** with usage-based expansion revenue. Managers subscribe to a base plan and purchase additional property slots as their portfolio grows. Pricing is structured to be accessible at entry level and scale naturally with the manager's business. Payment processing is handled via Stripe, with a built-in referral program that incentivizes word-of-mouth growth within the property management community.

## Competitive Advantage

- **AI-native from the start** — the Pillar Concierge reduces guest-to-manager communication without requiring manager involvement
- **Vertical-specific branding tools** built for operators who care about their professional image
- **Transparent, low-friction pricing** aligned to how property managers actually think about their portfolio size
